# Category brief: Best Robotics Plays

## Objective
Find the small/mid-cap public companies most likely to gain 200-500% within ~12 months because of the robotics buildout. Rank by expected multiple, probability-weighted, with liquidity Matt can actually trade (>= $500K/day, market cap $20M-$15B preferred).

## The stack (where the money flows)
Think in bill-of-materials terms. A humanoid or mobile manipulator needs, per unit:
- **Actuators**: frameless motors, planetary / harmonic / cycloidal reducers, ball screws, roller screws, drivers. Reducers and screws are the classic bottleneck; content per humanoid is high.
- **Sensing**: force/torque sensors, tactile skin, joint encoders, IMUs, cameras, depth, lidar (industrial and mobile robots), radar.
- **Hands**: dexterous hands, tendons, micro-actuators, tactile arrays.
- **Power**: batteries and cells sized for humanoids, power electronics, wireless charging.
- **Materials**: rare-earth magnets (NdFeB), specialty alloys, carbon fiber, precision gears/bearings.
- **Compute**: edge inference chips/modules, connectivity, thermal.
- **Brains/data**: simulation, teleoperation data, foundation models for robots, safety software.
- **Integrators and deployers**: warehouse/logistics robots, delivery robots, surgical robots, agriculture, defense/drones, inspection, cleaning, security. Recurring revenue per robot (RaaS) is a valuable model.
- **Picks-and-shovels**: test equipment, machine vision, motion control, factory automation, semicap serving robot chips.

## What a 3-5x candidate looks like here
- A **supplier with a design win** (or credible path to one) at Tesla Optimus, Figure, Agility, Apptronik, 1X, Unitree, Boston Dynamics, Amazon, or a major integrator, where the content-per-unit x plausible unit volume implies revenue several times the current market cap.
- **Capacity expansion announced before revenue shows up** (capex, new plant, hiring) - the market often waits for the P&L.
- **Underfollowed**: no or few analysts, low retail attention, but real disclosures.
- **Balance sheet that survives the wait**: net cash or funded runway; no ATM dependence.
- **Insider or strategic buying**, 13D filings, customer equity stakes.
- **A dated catalyst** within 6 months (contract, product, earnings inflection, conference demo with a named customer).
- Also valid: a **momentum breakout** in a robotics name with a real story (Matt's parabolic playbook), but say explicitly that it is a momentum play.
